# ScamShield: demo video script (about 2.5 minutes)

**Before recording**
- Run `npm run dev` (or open the live demo) in a clean browser window, zoomed to about 125% so text is readable.
- Set the language to English. Open WhatsApp Web or a phone screenshot of a scam message for scene 6.
- Record with OBS, Loom or QuickTime (screen + mic). Speak slowly and keep the mouse still while talking.

---

### Scene 1: The hook (0:00–0:15)
**Show:** The ScamShield home page.

> "Last year, Indians lost thousands of crores to online scams: fake KYC messages, parcel fees, job offers, even 'digital arrests'. The people hit hardest are our parents and grandparents, who often can't tell a fake message from a real one. So we built ScamShield."

### Scene 2: What it is (0:15–0:25)
**Show:** Slowly move over the header and the text box.

> "ScamShield is a free web app. You paste any suspicious SMS, WhatsApp message, email or link, and it tells you in plain language whether it's a scam, and why."

### Scene 3: Check a real-looking scam (0:25–0:55)
**Do:** Click the **🏦 Bank KYC** example. Scroll to the red result.

> "Here's a typical message: 'Your SBI account will be blocked today, update your KYC.' One click, and ScamShield says **Likely a scam**, 100 out of 100 risk."

**Do:** Point at the highlighted text, then scroll through the warning cards.

> "It highlights exactly which words are suspicious, then explains each warning sign simply. This link *mentions* SBI but isn't SBI's real website. The message threatens you, it rushes you, and it uses a cheap '.xyz' address. Banks never do any of that."

### Scene 4: What to do + warn family (0:55–1:15)
**Do:** Scroll to "What you should do" and the green WhatsApp button.

> "It doesn't just warn you. It tells you what to do next, including India's cybercrime helpline, 1930. And with one tap, you can send a warning to your family on WhatsApp, because if one person gets this message, their parents probably will too."

### Scene 5: It also says when things are fine (1:15–1:25)
**Do:** Click **✅ Normal message**.

> "It also doesn't panic over normal messages. A regular order update comes back as safe."

### Scene 6: Screenshot upload (1:25–1:45)
**Do:** Click **📷 Upload a screenshot** and pick a screenshot of a scam message.

> "Many older users can't copy text out of WhatsApp, but everyone knows how to take a screenshot. Upload it, and ScamShield reads the text right on your device, in English or Hindi, and checks it."

### Scene 7: Hindi (1:45–2:00)
**Do:** Click **हिन्दी**, then the **🇮🇳 Hindi KYC** example.

> "Most scam victims aren't comfortable in English, so the whole app works in Hindi too. It detects scams written in Hindi and Hinglish and explains them in Hindi."

### Scene 8: Spot the Scam quiz (2:00–2:20)
**Do:** Switch back to English. Open **🎯 Spot the scam quiz**, answer two questions.

> "The best protection is learning to spot scams yourself. Our quiz mixes real messages with scams. Notice that genuine bank alerts and OTP messages are in here too, so people learn the difference instead of just being scared of everything."

### Scene 9: Privacy, tech and close (2:20–2:40)
**Show:** The footer ("Private by design"), then the GitHub repo.

> "Everything runs inside your browser. Your messages are never uploaded anywhere, and there's no sign-up. It's built with React and a custom detection engine with automated tests. Next, we want to add more Indian languages and a WhatsApp bot. ScamShield: think before you tap. Thank you!"

- Keep it under 3 minutes. Cut pauses when editing.
- Add captions; judges often watch on mute.
